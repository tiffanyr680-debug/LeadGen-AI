Quick Start Guide

Step 1: Prospect Discovery

Go to the "Find Prospects" tab. Enter your target industry and city. Click Search. Our engine will locate top-rated businesses in that area.

Step 2: CRM Management

Click "Save Lead" on any business that fits your target profile. This moves them to your private local CRM where you can manage follow-ups.

Step 3: AI Cold Outreach

Select a lead and click "Draft Email." Our AI will generate a personalized message. Copy the text and paste it into your email provider to send.
